TA Final Exam Proctoring Scheduler

Each semester, upload the final exam routine and the course list. The app reads both, maps every course to its exam slot, and sends the result to the allocator. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Why is the class time needed?

The course list says when each section normally meets (for example ST 08:00 AM - 09:30 AM). The exam routine says which date those classes sit their final. The app joins the two using class days + class time, so every exam day below lists the class times that sit on it.

The exam time is the same as the class time unless you type a different one (for example 06:00 PM classes sitting at 05:00 PM; the time-shift rule below does that automatically).

Check the extracted routine, or enter it by hand: one block per exam day (class days, date, then every time slot that sits that day). The weekday fills in by itself.

Exam calendar follows your exam routine

Importing the exam routine fills this in by itself, with whatever class days (RA, ST, MW, …) and time slots your routine has. Without a routine, set the dates by hand. Every TA's Pick button then offers one-tap time slots for these days.

TA roster and personal exams

Faculty initials are comma-separated (ABC, XYZ). Click Pick in the personal-exams column to choose exam slots with one tap (several per day is fine), or type them yourself, separated by ; like 2026-12-10 | 09:00-11:00; 2026-12-12 | 14:00-16:00.
